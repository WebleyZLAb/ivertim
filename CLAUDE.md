## Project Summary

**Updated 2026-09-02 — see Session Notes for the full pivot.** Ivertim is a Prijedor-based company now positioned primarily as a board-cutting service center ("drvni centar"): (1) service cutting, edge-banding and drilling of board materials — primary, capacity-first — and (2) made-to-measure panel furniture (kitchens, wardrobes, cabinets, etc.) — secondary, still fully offered but subordinate to cutting-service capacity. Full apartment/house fit-outs still happen but are not a separately marketed service (saturated referral network). 3D design, CNC production, transport and installation remain part of the delivery *process* for both services, not separate line items.

Brand essence: **"Kompetentnost kojoj vjeruješ"** (Competence you can trust). Tone: calm, precise, advisory, never salesy or superlative-driven. Verbal principle: *Slušaj → postavi → shvati → izmjeri → predloži* (Listen → ask → understand → measure → propose).

Primary audience: new apartment/house owners, buyers wanting full fit-outs, small carpentry workshops needing service-cutting. Architects are explicitly not the primary target in phase one.
